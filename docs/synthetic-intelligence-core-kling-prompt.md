## Hauptprompt

> Use the supplied image as an immutable identity reference for one single,
> self-contained synthetic intelligence core. Preserve its exact compact outer
> silhouette, proportions, construction, obsidian-black ceramic armor,
> integrated aged-gold mechanisms, deep wine-red computational tissue and all
> existing surface details.
>
> There is exactly one object in the entire video. The object must remain one
> permanently connected solid body in every frame. Never generate anything
> outside its existing compact silhouette. No pieces may detach, emerge, grow,
> disappear, orbit or float around it.
>
> The complete object remains centered, fully visible and at exactly the same
> scale. The camera is perfectly locked with no camera orbit, zoom, pan or
> perspective movement.
>
> During the ten-second shot, the complete compact object performs exactly one
> slow and continuous 360-degree rotation around its own slightly tilted
> vertical axis. The rotation has constant, heavy cinematic momentum. Reveal a
> coherent rear side made from the same materials and construction. Preserve
> one compact connected silhouette from every angle. No shape change, no
> morphing, no opening, no expansion and no deformation.
>
> Only the concealed mechanisms inside the existing apertures may move very
> subtly. Integrated gold lamellae rotate with precise clockwork motion. A
> restrained warm-gold neural pulse travels only through the existing lines
> embedded in the body. The deep wine-red tissue performs one nearly invisible
> contraction and release. All animation remains strictly inside the original
> body.
>
> Add a minimal weightless hover of only a few pixels while keeping the object's
> center locked. The final frame returns precisely to the starting position,
> scale, front orientation, illumination and internal mechanism state, forming
> a seamless ten-second loop.
>
> Keep the background perfectly uniform solid chroma green #00FF00. No
> gradient, shadows, reflections, floor, horizon, texture or lighting variation
> on the background. No green spill on the object.
